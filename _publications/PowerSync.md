excerpt: 'Achieving carbon neutrality requires energy-efficient serverless computing, yet co-hosted functions are highly variable and sensitive to power capping. We propose PowerSync, a synchronization-based framework that aligns application power behavior to enable consistent operation on shared hosts. By exploiting uniform power patterns, PowerSync improves energy efficiency by up to 16% without performance loss compared to conventional strategies.'
