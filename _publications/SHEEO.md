excerpt: 'Autonomous embedded systems face challenges in sustaining energy efficiency under stochastic runtime variance, especially on resource-constrained platforms. We present SHEEO, an optimizer that leverages shadow cycles across heterogeneous resources for variability-aware power management. SHEEO monitors variances and applies a two-phase reinforcement learning scheme. On a commercial edge platform, it harvests up to 88% shadow cycles and improves energy efficiency by 39% over state-of-the-art methods with negligible overhead.'
