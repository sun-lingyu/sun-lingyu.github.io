excerpt: 'Real-time perception is critical for autonomous driving, where a large fusion-based Bird’s-Eye-View (BEV) DNN collaborates with auxiliary Perspective-View (PV) models. On GPU-integrated dual SoCs, allocating GPU resources to meet distinct real-time requirements with latency predictability remains unsolved. We propose Jigsaw, a timeline management framework for BEV-centric perception. Jigsaw exploits component parallelism to reduce BEV latency and leverages idle GPU timeslots: stable slots for hard real-time PV tasks and unstable slots for soft real-time ones. Implemented on a dual-SoC prototype, Jigsaw achieves 1.52–1.63× speedup for BEV models and ensures PV deadlines without compromising BEV predictability.'
