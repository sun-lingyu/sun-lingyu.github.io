excerpt: 'Multi-modal DNNs surpass uni-modal models in diverse applications but remain underexplored at the system and architecture level. Existing benchmarks focus on uni-modal or algorithmic aspects, lacking comprehensive workload characterization. We present MM-Bench, an open-source benchmark suite of real-world multi-modal DNN workloads with system-level metrics. Using MM-Bench, we analyze their unique traits—multi-stage execution, frequent synchronization, and high heterogeneity—and extend evaluation to edge devices. Our results provide insights for future software and hardware optimizations supporting multi-modal DNNs across cloud and edge platforms.'
